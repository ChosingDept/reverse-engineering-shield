# Disclaimer

Reverse Engineering Shield is a defensive tool. It helps you reduce what your own
software reveals and detect what your own builds and sites expose.

- It does not guarantee protection. Determined analysts can still read client code;
  the guidance aims to make that reading worthless, not impossible.
- Use the URL mode only on systems you own or are explicitly authorized to test.
- Nothing here is legal advice. Laws on reverse engineering, scraping and software
  protection vary by jurisdiction; consult a lawyer.
